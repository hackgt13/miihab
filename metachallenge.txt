MiiHab is an AI-powered social rehabilitation platform for patients recovering from injuries. 
Through motion-controlled games in a shared virtual environment, patients can exercise and work 
toward recovery goals.

MiiHab strengthens human connection by allowing patients to exercise together, celebrate milestones, 
and build friendships with others on similar recovery journeys. It transforms an often isolating recovery 
process into a connected, supportive experience.

AI provides personalized movement feedback and matches patients with compatible rehabilitation buddies
based on shared goals and progress, making rehabilitation more adaptive, engaging, and social.
