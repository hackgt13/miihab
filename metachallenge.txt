MiiHab is an AI-powered social rehabilitation platform for patients recovering from injuries. 
Through motion-controlled games in a shared virtual environment, patients can exercise together
and work toward recovery goals.

MiiHab strengthens human connection by helping patients build friendships with others on similar 
recovery journeys. Patients can exercise together, celebrate milestones, and stay connected through 
AI-generated conversation recaps, suggested replies, and personalized check-ins. Milestone cards can 
also be shared through Facebook or Messenger, transforming an often isolating recovery process into
a supportive social experience.

AI provides personalized movement feedback and matches patients with compatible rehabilitation buddies 
based on shared goals and progress. Powered by Meta's Muse Spark through the Meta Model API, MiiHab
generates personalized social interactions that help patients maintain friendships between rehabilitation 
sessions, making recovery more adaptive, engaging, and connected.
