How this capture works

Pose runs in this browser with MediaPipe. Only landmarks go to the local Mac bridge, where they are saved; video never leaves the browser and nothing is uploaded.

“Joints in view” means the exercise’s reference joints are inside the image with visibility ≥ 0.5. It checks framing, not measurement accuracy. Teal landmarks meet the threshold; amber ones are low or unknown. The image is not mirrored, and left/right follow MediaPipe’s subject labels.

Source video time and inference time are stored separately; camera exposure timestamps are not available in the browser.
