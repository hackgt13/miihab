How capture works

Pose runs in this browser with MediaPipe. Only landmarks go to the bridge on this Mac, where they are saved. Video never leaves the browser.

“Joints in view” means the exercise’s reference joints are inside the image with visibility ≥ 0.5. It checks framing, not measurement accuracy. Blue landmarks meet the threshold; coral ones are low or unknown. The image is not mirrored; left and right follow MediaPipe’s subject labels.

Source video time and inference time are stored separately; the browser does not expose camera exposure timestamps.
