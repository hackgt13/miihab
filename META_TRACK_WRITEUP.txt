MiiHab — Meta Track: Bringing People Closer Together with AI
==============================================================

WHO IT IS FOR

MiiHab is for people recovering at home from injury, surgery or a neurological event such as a spinal
cord injury: people doing rehab seated, often alone, between visits. About 1.7 billion people live with
a musculoskeletal condition, the world's leading cause of disability. A physical therapist we interviewed
told us most patients get about one hour a week with their therapist. That leaves 167 hours with no
feedback, no company, and no one noticing when they stop.

It is also for the people around them: the friends who want to help but don't know how, other patients
working toward the same goal, and the physical therapist who sees none of those 167 hours.

MiiHab puts the patient in a Meta Quest headset, uses the AirPods they already own as motion sensors, and
turns rehab into a place they go with other people: a movement studio, a golf course, a bowling alley.


HOW IT STRENGTHENS CONNECTION

- With a coach: Alex, a voiced physical-therapy coach, sits beside the patient in VR and speaks to them
  about every rep, using what was actually measured: their reach, their pace, whether their trunk leaned.
- With friends: patients rehab side by side in a group session, play golf and bowling together, and
  message each other. Muse suggests the friend to reach out to today ("returned after four days"), drafts
  a reply the patient can send in one press, and turns a streak into a milestone card they can share.
- With strangers who understand: MiiHab introduces two patients working toward the same goal, anonymously
  and only on a double opt-in. Muse writes the line that explains why they might want to meet, from their
  shared goals alone: no names, no diagnoses, no numbers.
- With the world outside the app: through Meta's share dialog (Graph API platform), a patient can share a
  milestone to Facebook or Messenger. The patient picks the audience; the app never posts on its own.
- With their clinician: the physical therapist sees the 167 hours live in an EHR, reads what the coach
  said, and changes the plan. The patient's headset then explains the change in the coach's voice.


WHY AI IS ESSENTIAL

Recovery at home fails because it is lonely and unguided. Neither problem can be solved by a human being
present for all 167 hours, and neither can be solved by a static app.

THE COACH'S BRAIN IS META'S MUSE SPARK. Alex hears the patient through ElevenLabs and speaks with an
ElevenLabs voice, but every word Alex chooses, and every tool Alex calls, is decided by Muse Spark
(muse-spark-1.3). Tools include reading the measured reps, reading the patient's plan, and relaying a
message to the care team. Muse receives the live measurements from the patient's own sensors and turns
them into encouragement and correction that a patient can act on mid-set: "72 degrees with a steady
trunk. Reach a little higher toward 85 on the next one." A rule-based coach cannot hold a conversation,
notice that someone sounds discouraged, or explain a plan change in plain words. A person cannot be there
at 9 pm on a Sunday. Muse can.

Muse is also what makes the social layer human rather than mechanical. It decides whom to spotlight,
summarises a conversation the patient hasn't opened in a while, drafts the first message back, introduces
two strangers by what they share, and gives a quiet achievement the words that make it worth sharing.
These are small moments, and they are the ones that keep people coming back and keep them in touch.

AI in MiiHab narrates and suggests; it never acts for the patient. It never sends a message, never shares
anything, and never changes the plan. Every connection is a choice the patient makes, with AI making that
choice easier to make.


BUILT WITH META

- Meta Quest 2: the patient's first-person view, head tracking for trunk lean, and the in-headset coach
  voice.
- Muse Spark (Meta Model API): Alex's brain, plus the friends spotlight, recaps, reply drafts,
  introductions and milestone cards.
- Meta share dialog (Graph API platform, app id 1313986181801600): sharing milestones to Facebook and
  Messenger.
